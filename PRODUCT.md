# NoteFlow
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Turn videos, transcripts and documents into source-linked study notes. The current user priority is to preserve important information and make failures, limits and progress understandable.

## Operating Context
The app is live on Vercel, with Supabase authentication and private Turso storage. The user requested a NoteGPT-style start/login experience and a side-by-side study workspace, while keeping NoteFlow branding and every existing additional tool working.

## Capabilities and Constraints
YouTube, text, PDF and subtitle imports; saved summaries, transcript, personal notes, chat, insights, mind maps, flashcards, quizzes, slides, translation and exports. Free-launch limits remain enforced. External AI availability cannot be guaranteed. No owned training model, visual video understanding, OCR or unattended background queue is implemented. Email verification stays disabled by user choice.

## Evidence on Hand
Public NoteGPT screenshot and bounded public-bundle REA evidence in ignored audit/reference; local application tests and isolated browser fixtures. No competitor server source or private prompts are available. No specific failing video or missing-fact checklist has yet been supplied.
